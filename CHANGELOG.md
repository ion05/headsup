# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-03

First public release.

- Full-screen alert on every display for upcoming calendar events, on a frosted blur with Liquid Glass controls (macOS 26+).
- Join button for Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams links. `Return` joins, `Esc` dismisses.
- Snooze for 1, 5 or 10 minutes, or until the event starts.
- Events starting at the same time share one alert.
- Per-calendar alert times: 10 minutes before, 1 minute before, at start.
- Turn whole accounts or single calendars on and off.
- Private events: hide details per event, per calendar, or everywhere with one switch.
- Auto-hide event details when you're not on a trusted Wi-Fi network.
- Menu-bar countdown to your next event and an agenda panel with Join buttons.
- Skips all-day, declined and canceled events.
- Works with any account Apple Calendar can see (iCloud, Google, Exchange/Outlook). No sign-in, no servers.
- Open at login.
- Automatic updates, which can be turned off in Settings.
